FAQ

Questions clients often ask

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

What does Qorvex do?

Qorvex is an AI automation and software development company based in India. We build AI automations and chatbots, business websites, e-commerce stores, custom software, and Android and iOS apps, and we set up and support cloud infrastructure on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud — for startups and growing businesses worldwide.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so we quote after a short discovery call. You get a clear, itemised estimate before any work begins — no hidden charges.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

It depends on scope and features. After understanding your requirements, we share a realistic timeline with milestones so you always know what's next.

Will I own the code and the website?

Yes. Once the project is delivered, the source code, designs, domains and accounts belong to you.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, updates, monitoring and improvements, so your product keeps running smoothly as your business grows.

Can you work with clients outside India?

Absolutely. We work remotely with clients worldwide and keep communication simple with regular calls, demos and progress updates.
