1. Who we are

Qorvex ("Qorvex", "we", "us", "our") is a technology services company based in India that works remotely with clients worldwide. We provide AI automation, business website development, e-commerce websites, custom software development, mobile app development, and cloud & deployment support.

For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Qorvex is the data fiduciary for the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill in the contact form on this website, email us or call us, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • The project details and any other information you choose to share in your message

Please don't include sensitive information such as passwords, bank or card details, or health information in the contact form.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services that deliver fonts and code to this page automatically receive basic technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages requested and the date and time of the request. This is used to deliver the website, keep it secure and fix problems.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics tools on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To prepare proposals, estimates and contracts you ask for
  • To deliver, support and maintain the services you engage us for
  • To send project updates and service-related messages
  • To keep this website secure and working properly
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations

We will not send you marketing emails unless you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Consent and legal basis

By submitting the contact form or contacting us, you consent to us processing the information you share for the purposes listed above. We also process information where it is needed to perform a contract with you, or to comply with the law.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing consent won't affect anything we did before you withdrew it, but we may no longer be able to provide some services.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run this website and our business, and only as much as they need:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) — delivers contact form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) — hosts our business email, where enquiries are received and answered.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and processes technical request data.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — deliver the fonts and 3D graphics code used on this page, and receive your IP address when they do.

Some of these providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the duration of our engagement. After that, we keep records only as long as required for legal, tax or accounting purposes, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

7. How we protect it

This website is served over a secure HTTPS connection. We limit access to personal information to the people who need it to do their work, and use reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and how we use it
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity

To use any of these rights, email us at enquiry.qorvex@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies or use tracking cookies. Third-party services that deliver fonts and code may receive standard technical information as described above.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.
